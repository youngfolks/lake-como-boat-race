Lake Como Boat Racing

Race varnished mahogany runabouts across 1950s Lake Como: along the Como waterfront inside the breakwater, past Villa d'Este and Villa Carlotta, through the channel behind Isola Comacina, round Bellagio's point and under the stone arches of the Ponte Azzone Visconti at Lecco.

Play the Primo Bacino course free in your browser. The Full Race Season, with the Centro Lago and Ramo di Lecco courses, is in the iPhone app.

Inspired by the Centomiglia del Lario, raced on the lake every year since 1949.
