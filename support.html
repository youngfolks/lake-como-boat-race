How to play

  • Your boat accelerates on its own. Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer.
  • Hold Brake (bottom right) to slow down; keep holding to reverse.
  • Skim close to marker buoys, fly through gold rings and land ramp jumps to charge your boost, then tap Boost (bottom left).
  • Pass the gates in order. The blue guide on the water shows the way round.
  • With a keyboard: arrow keys steer, down or Space brakes, up boosts, P pauses.

Full Race Season

Primo Bacino, the Como basin, is free. The Full Race Season is a one-time purchase that unlocks every other course, Centro Lago and Ramo di Lecco, and is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Restore Purchases: choose a locked course on the main menu, tap Quick Race, then tap Restore Purchases.

Racing a friend

Race a Friend uses Game Center. Both phones need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings › Game Center) and online; the host's course is used for the race.

No sound?

Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone. Like most games, Lake Como Boat Racing stays quiet when the phone is set to silent.
