Effective September 26, 2026

The short version

Lake Como Boat Racing does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The one thing that ever leaves your phone is an anonymous crash report, described below.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your iPhone so it works the way you left it: your best race times, your sound settings, the course you last picked, and whether you have bought the Full Race Season. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Crash and problem reports

If the game crashes, or a two-player race fails to connect, the app sends a technical report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so the problem can be fixed. A report contains the device model, iOS version, app version and what the game was doing at the time. It contains no name, email address, Apple Account, location or contacts, and the service is set not to store your IP address. Reports are never used for advertising or tracking.

Two-player races

Racing a friend uses Apple's Game Center. Apple matches the two players and passes the boat positions between the two phones; nothing goes through a server of ours. Your Game Center nickname is shown to your opponent during the race. Game Center is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Full Race Season is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
